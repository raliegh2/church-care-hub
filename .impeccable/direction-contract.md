# Direction contract: Welcome Desk Handoff

## THESIS

Church care reads as one composed handoff register, not a dashboard of floating cards. The interface owns calm operational continuity and refuses the category-default pile of colorful status containers.

## OWN-WORLD

Deep evergreen owns navigation, trust, and primary actions. SDA yellow is confined to the church mark, current-record wash, and guidance states. Ivory paper, white fields, dark ink, mineral rules, restrained corners, and low shadows carry the rest.

## STORY

An usher finds a person, understands the current care state, records a visit or support need, and sees the history without losing context. Literal labels and ordered sections keep the ministry handoff clear.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

Desktop joins a compact directory to a wider record pane under one edge; the left rail stays evergreen through the content height, and the small Cyventura credit closes the bottom-right. Mobile stacks directory, selected record, actions, and history with no horizontal overflow.

## FORM

Chosen form: welcome-desk care register, first on the ordered direction list. Concept seed key: `cc0248df`. A shared yellow dot travels to the selected row while its wash changes immediately; reduced-motion preferences remove that travel.
