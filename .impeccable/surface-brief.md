# Surface brief: People care workspace

- Preserve every current permission, action, route, care-note workflow, visit workflow, filter, and empty state.
- Do not read, export, seed, rewrite, delete, or submit visitor records during implementation or verification.
- Replace the preferred-name input with a nullable visitor address input. Retain the legacy database column and any existing values unchanged.
- Make visitor rows and the selected record feel like one professional register, not a pile of cards.
- Keep all essential controls usable at 320px and all editable fields at least 16px on mobile.
- Use only synthetic records in visual verification.
